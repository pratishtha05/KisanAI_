COTTON (Gossypium spp.) - CROP OVERVIEW

Growing Season and Climate:
Cotton is a long-duration kharif crop, sown between April and May (irrigated) or May-June (rainfed), and harvested in multiple pickings from October through January. It requires a long frost-free period (180-200 days), warm temperatures (21-30°C) and moderate rainfall, with a dry spell during boll maturation and picking to prevent boll rot and quality loss.

Soil Requirements:
Deep, well-drained black cotton soils (regur) or medium loamy soils are ideal, with good water-holding capacity but no waterlogging. pH range of 6.0-8.0 is suitable. Cotton has a deep taproot system and performs poorly in shallow or compacted soils.

Seed Rate and Sowing:
For Bt cotton hybrids, seed rate is typically 1.5-2.0 kg/hectare, sown at a spacing of 90-120 cm between rows and 45-60 cm between plants, depending on hybrid vigour and soil fertility.

Growth Stages:
1. Germination and seedling establishment (0-25 days)
2. Vegetative/squaring stage (25-60 days) - square (flower bud) formation begins
3. Flowering stage (60-100 days) - peak flowering and boll set
4. Boll development and maturation (100-150 days)
5. Boll opening and picking (150-200+ days, multiple pickings over 3-4 months)

Common Varieties:
Bt cotton hybrids dominate cultivation (e.g., RCH-2, Bunny Bt, MRC-7017 Bt) selected for regional pest resistance, particularly against bollworm complex.

Water Management:
Cotton is relatively drought-tolerant once established but is sensitive to moisture stress during flowering and boll development. Excess irrigation or waterlogging, particularly in the vegetative stage, promotes excessive vegetative growth at the expense of boll set and increases susceptibility to root rot.

Pest Pressure:
Cotton is highly susceptible to sucking pests (whitefly, jassids, aphids, thrips) and bollworms; Bt cotton reduces bollworm pressure but not sucking pest pressure, requiring integrated pest management throughout the season.

Yield Expectations:
Under good irrigation and pest management, seed cotton (kapas) yields of 20-25 quintals/hectare are achievable; rainfed cotton yields significantly lower (8-12 quintals/hectare).

Harvesting:
Cotton is picked manually in 3-4 rounds as bolls open over the season, since bolls do not mature uniformly. Picking should be done in dry weather to maintain lint quality and avoid contamination.
